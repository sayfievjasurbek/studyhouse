After completing a degree, F-1 graduates can apply for up to 12 months of Optional Practical Training. Graduates of eligible STEM degrees can apply for a 24-month extension through an E-Verify employer.

Visa path

F-1 Student Visa. The Gateway to the USA.

Students who study full-time at a US university normally need an F-1 visa. The university issues the Form I-20 that starts the process.

  1. 01Receive your admission offer and Form I-20 from the university
  2. 02Pay the I-901 SEVIS fee (US$350)
  3. 03Complete the online DS-160 form and pay the visa application fee (US$185)
  4. 04Book your visa interview at a US embassy or consulate
  5. 05Attend the interview with your I-20, passport and proof of funds
  6. 06Visa approved — you may enter the US up to 30 days before your program start date

Questions parents ask

What families ask before choosing a US university

Honest answers — including what Study House cannot promise.

What will studying in the USA really cost per year?

Official 2026–27 estimates for international undergraduates at the universities in our table run from about US$62,000 to US$86,000 a year including tuition, housing, food, books and insurance. Tuition alone is US$32,104–57,484 at the five universities with published figures. Several universities offer international undergraduates little or no scholarship money, so check the table before you shortlist.

Is it safe? What should we check?

Study House cannot give a safety rating for a city or campus. US universities publish campus security information and run international student offices — check these for each shortlisted university, follow your government’s travel advice, and ask us to help you compare cities on cost and student support.

What if the visa is refused?

A refusal is not always the end. The embassy gives a reason, and you may be able to reapply with stronger evidence, for example clearer proof of funds or of your study plan. Visa application fees are generally not refunded. We can go through the refusal reason with you, but the embassy makes the final decision.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Study House cannot guarantee admission or a visa. Universities decide admission and embassies decide visas. What we do is help you choose realistic universities, prepare a complete application and avoid mistakes that can be avoided.

How do we stay in contact with you?

Leave your phone number or Telegram username in the booking form and we will contact you through it. At your first consultation, tell us how and how often you want to receive updates.

Can I work to pay for my studies?

Only in limited ways. F-1 students may work on campus for up to 20 hours a week during classes, and off-campus work is not allowed in the first academic year. Do not plan to pay tuition from work income — universities and visa officers expect proof that you can fund your studies.
