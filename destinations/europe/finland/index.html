Country Overview

The System That Lets You Work 30 Hours a Week — and Stay Two Years After Graduating.

Finland introduced tuition fees for non-EU students in 2017, but most universities offer partial-to-full waivers for high-achieving applicants, and Finnish/Swedish-taught programmes remain free for every nationality. Combined with a two-year post-study jobseeker permit, Finland offers one of Europe's clearest long-term pathways.

Visa Pathway

Residence Permit for Studies. The Gateway to Finland.

Non-EU students need a Residence Permit for Studies before arrival, applied for online via Enter Finland — it's recommended to apply as early as possible given longer processing times.

  1. 01University acceptance received
  2. 02Residence permit application submitted via Enter Finland
  3. 03Proof of funds & tuition payment confirmed
  4. 04Documents & health insurance prepared
  5. 05Biometrics enrolled at nearest Finnish mission
  6. 06Permit granted — valid for the full duration of studies
