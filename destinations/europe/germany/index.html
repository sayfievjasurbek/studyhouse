Country Overview

The Only System in the World Where Excellence Is Free.

German state universities charge a semester contribution of approximately €100–€400 to cover administrative costs. Tuition itself is zero. This is not a scholarship — it is the default. The degree you receive is identical to one paid for privately anywhere else in the world.

The Study House Approach

The German System Rewards Precision. We Provide It.

THE DOCUMENT

Every document submitted to a German institution must meet Anabin verification standards. We manage certified translation, notarisation, and Apostille end-to-end.

Visa Pathway

National Visa Type D. The Gateway to Germany.

Every non-EU student requires a National Visa Type D before entering Germany. It requires a blocked account, an acceptance letter, and a consular appointment.

  1. 01University acceptance letter secured
  2. 02Blocked account opened — minimum €12,000 deposited
  3. 03Documents translated, notarised, and Apostilled
  4. 04Visa application filed through the German Embassy
  5. 05Consular interview preparation completed
  6. 06Visa granted — residence permit filed within 14 days
