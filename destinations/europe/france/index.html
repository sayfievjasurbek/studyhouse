Country Overview

The System Where a World-Class Degree Costs a Fraction of the Alternatives.

Non-EU students at French public universities pay a differentiated rate of roughly €2,895/year for a bachelor's and €3,941/year for a master's — far below EU/EEA equivalents at private universities elsewhere, and many students qualify for exemptions that bring this even lower.

Visa Pathway

VLS-TS. The Gateway to France.

Full-degree non-EU students need the VLS-TS long-stay student visa, which functions as a residence permit and must be validated online within 3 months of arrival.

  1. 01Campus France (Études en France) account created
  2. 02University applications submitted through the portal
  3. 03Acceptance secured & proof of funds arranged (~€615/month)
  4. 04Documents translated and legalised
  5. 05Visa application filed via France-Visas / VFS Global
  6. 06Visa granted — validated online via ANEF within 3 months of arrival
