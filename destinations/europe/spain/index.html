Country Overview

The Country Where You Can Work While You Study — Automatically.

Since the 2025 reform, Spain's student visa automatically includes work authorization for up to 30 hours a week — no separate permit process. Combined with tuition that's a fraction of Anglophone destinations, Spain offers one of Europe's best study-to-work pathways.

Visa Pathway

The National Visa (Type D). The Gateway to Spain.

Non-EU students need a National Visa (Type D) for courses over 90 days, followed by a residence card (TIE) after arrival.

  1. 01University acceptance letter secured
  2. 02Proof of funds arranged (~€600/month)
  3. 03Documents translated, notarised, and Apostilled
  4. 04Visa application filed at the Spanish Consulate
  5. 05Consular interview and biometrics completed
  6. 06Visa granted — TIE residence card collected within 30 days of arrival
