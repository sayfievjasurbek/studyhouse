Country Overview

The Only System Where Your Tuition Reflects Your Means, Not Your Nationality.

Italian public universities set tuition through the ISEE means-testing system — students from lower-income households can pay close to nothing, while the standard flat rate for others ranges up to roughly €4,600/year. It's one of the fairest tuition systems in Europe, open equally to EU and non-EU applicants.

THE BRIDGE

Universitaly Pre-Enrollment Strategy

Every non-EU applicant must pre-enroll through the Universitaly portal before applying for a visa. We manage the portal submission and university validation end-to-end.

THE FINANCE

ISEE & Proof-of-Funds Management

THE DOCUMENT

Visa Pathway

National Visa Type D. The Gateway to Italy.

Non-EU students need a National Visa Type D (Visto per Studio) before entering Italy, followed by a residence permit application within 8 days of arrival.

  1. 01Universitaly pre-enrollment completed
  2. 02University acceptance & Declaration of Value obtained
  3. 03Proof of funds arranged (~€8,000/year)
  4. 04Documents translated and legalised
  5. 05Visa application filed at the Italian Consulate
  6. 06Visa granted — Permesso di Soggiorno filed within 8 days of arrival
