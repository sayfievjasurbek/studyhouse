Chevening Scholarship

A fully funded one-year master’s in the UK, for professionals.

Book a Consultation →

Chevening is funded by the UK’s Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office and its partner organisations. Uzbekistan is an eligible country. It is aimed at people who already have around two years of work experience, and you apply on chevening.org yourself.

Level
A one-year taught master’s at a UK university
Work experience
2,800 hours, acquired after your undergraduate degree
Funding
Tuition, a monthly living allowance, economy return flights, an arrival allowance and the visa cost
Deadline
6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC, for studies starting in autumn 2027

Are You Eligible?

  • You are a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country or territory. Uzbekistan is eligible.
  • You hold an undergraduate degree that qualifies you for a UK master’s programme.
  • You have completed 2,800 hours of work experience after your undergraduate degree — roughly two years at 35 hours a week. Full-time, part-time, remote, voluntary work and paid or unpaid internships all count.
  • You commit to returning to your country of award for at least two years after the scholarship ends.
  • There is no upper age limit.

How to Apply.

  1. 4 August 2026, 11:00 UTC Applications open
  2. 6 October 2026, 11:00 UTC Applications close — submissions are not accepted after this time
  3. March – April 2027 Interviews
  4. Mid-June 2027 onwards Results announced
  5. September / October 2027 Scholars begin their studies in the UK

These are the dates Chevening publishes for the 2026–27 application cycle, for awards beginning in the 2027–28 academic year.

Questions Parents Ask.

What exactly does Chevening pay for?

Tuition fees, invoiced to Chevening directly by your university; a monthly living allowance that differs inside and outside London; economy return flights by an approved route; an arrival allowance; the entry clearance (visa) cost; and excess baggage on the return flight.

What counts towards the 2,800 hours?

Full-time and part-time employment, remote work, voluntary work, and paid or unpaid internships, all acquired after you finished your undergraduate degree. You are eligible once you reach 2,800 hours, even if you reached them in under two years.

Is there a fee to apply?

Not verified yet — see the official site

Do I have to come back to Uzbekistan afterwards?

Yes. Committing to return to your country of award for at least two years after the scholarship ends is one of the eligibility criteria.

Can Study House get me a Chevening award?

No. Chevening selects its scholars, and the competition is decided by the Chevening Secretariat and the interview panel. We help you shape your essays and prepare for the interview.
