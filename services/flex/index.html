One academic year at an American high school, with an American host family.

FLEX is sponsored by the U.S. Department of State and administered by American Councils for International Education. Uzbekistan is one of the participating countries, and the programme is fully funded — you apply directly to American Councils, not through an agency.

Are You Eligible?

  • You are a citizen of Uzbekistan.
  • You are enrolled in a secondary school or a lyceum/college in Uzbekistan.
  • You meet the age criteria and class of study FLEX publishes for the current round.
  • You have excellent and good grades.
  • You have studied English and can demonstrate your English at registration.
  • You meet the requirements for a United States entry visa.

How to Apply.

  1. Round 1 Preliminary online application, including short essay questions, submitted within ten days of starting it
  2. Later rounds Further testing and interviews run by American Councils
  3. Selection Finalists are placed with a volunteer host family and a US high school
  4. Departure One academic year in the United States

American Councils publishes the round dates for each cycle and advises checking FLEX Uzbekistan’s own channels for current eligibility requirements and dates.

Questions Parents Ask.

Does FLEX cost anything?

No. American Councils lists FLEX as fully funded, sponsored by the U.S. Department of State. The official application is made directly to American Councils.

Is FLEX still running in Uzbekistan?

Yes. Uzbekistan is listed as a participating country on the FLEX programme site, and American Councils administers it in Uzbekistan.

When is the deadline?

Not verified yet — see the official site

Can Study House improve my chances?

We can help you understand the requirements and practise your English and your essays. We cannot influence the selection, and nobody who says they can is telling you the truth.

Where will I live?

Every FLEX student lives with a volunteer host family for one academic year and attends a US high school.
