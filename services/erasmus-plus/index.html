Erasmus+

Study in Europe through the EU’s Erasmus+ programme.

Book a Consultation →

Erasmus+ is not a single application. For a student in Uzbekistan there are two realistic routes, and they work very differently: an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master, which you apply to yourself, and international credit mobility, which is arranged by your own university.

Route 1
Erasmus Mundus Joint Master — you apply directly to the consortium running the course
Route 2
Credit mobility — arranged through your home university’s international office
Duration
Joint Masters run 12, 18 or 24 months (60/90/120 ECTS); credit mobility runs 2 to 12 months
Application period
Most Joint Masters take applications between October and January for the following academic year

Are You Eligible?

  • For an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master: you are a master’s-level applicant. The European Commission states these programmes are open to students from all over the world, with no nationality restriction.
  • Each consortium sets its own academic and language requirements — they are published on the course’s own website.
  • For credit mobility: you are already enrolled at a university in Uzbekistan that has an inter-institutional agreement with a European university.
  • Credit mobility lasts a minimum of 2 months and a maximum of 12 months within one study cycle.

How to Apply.

  1. Step 1 Decide which route applies to you: a Joint Master you apply to yourself, or credit mobility through your university
  2. Step 2 For a Joint Master, find the course in the official Erasmus Mundus catalogue and read the consortium’s own requirements
  3. Step 3 Apply directly to the consortium, usually between October and January
  4. Step 4 For credit mobility, contact the international or Erasmus+ office at your own university — there is no central application form
  5. Step 5 If you are selected, arrange your visa for the country where you will study

The European Commission states there is no central application form for Erasmus+ study mobility; students are told to contact the international or Erasmus+ office of their university.

Questions Parents Ask.

Can I apply to Erasmus+ directly as an individual?

For an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master, yes — you apply to the consortium that runs the course. For an exchange, no: credit mobility is arranged between universities, so it goes through your own university’s international office.

Does Erasmus+ pay for everything?

Not necessarily. For study mobility the European Commission says the grant contributes to travel and daily living costs, and the amount varies with distance and the cost of living in the destination. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters have their own scholarships, awarded by the consortium.

How long can I go for?

Credit mobility runs from a minimum of 2 months to a maximum of 12 months within one study cycle. Joint Masters run 12, 18 or 24 months.

When are the deadlines?

Most Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters take applications between October and January for courses starting the following academic year. Each consortium sets its own exact date.

Can Study House guarantee a place or a scholarship?

No. Selection is made by the consortium or by the universities involved. We help you build the application and meet the deadlines.
