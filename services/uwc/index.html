United World Colleges

Two years of the IB Diploma, on a campus with students from around the world.

UWC selects students through national committees rather than through the schools themselves. In Uzbekistan that committee is UWC Uzbekistan, and it runs its own application, test, interview and selection day before nominating students to a UWC school.

Level
Grade 9, 10 or 11 at the time of applying — not yet a university student
Duration
Two years: the IB Diploma Programme 2027–2029
Funding
More than 80% of UWC students receive financial support; a financial needs assessment is part of the selection
Application period
1 August – 30 September, through UWC Uzbekistan

Are You Eligible?

  • You are a citizen of Uzbekistan.
  • You are a student in the 9th, 10th or 11th grade of a secondary school or post-secondary institution.
  • You are 16 or 17 years old as of 1 September, the start of the academic year.
  • You speak fluent English, or you can learn it quickly.
  • You are a highly capable student with a strong academic record.

How to Apply.

  1. 1 August – 30 September Online application
  2. 2 – 6 October Online logical reasoning test
  3. November Online video interview
  4. November Final selection day
  5. December Financial needs assessment
  6. January – June Nominations to UWC schools
  7. July Orientation course

UWC Uzbekistan publishes this as a preliminary timeline for the 2027–2029 programme and notes that exact dates can change.

Questions Parents Ask.

Do I choose which UWC school I go to?

No. You apply to the UWC Uzbekistan national committee, and the committee nominates selected students to a UWC school. Applying through a national committee is one of the three routes UWC lists; the others are the Global Selection Programme and the International Application Pathway.

Is there a fee to apply?

Not verified yet — see the official site

Will I get a scholarship?

UWC states that more than 80% of its students receive financial support from its donors and partners, and UWC Uzbekistan runs a financial needs assessment in December. Nobody can tell you in advance what your award will be.

Can Study House get me selected?

No. The selection is made by the UWC Uzbekistan committee and we have no part in it. We help you prepare a strong, honest application and practise for the interview.

What if I am in the 12th grade or already at university?

Then you are not eligible for this route — the committee requires that you are not yet a university student when you apply.
