What Goes Wrong, and What We Do About It

From Application to Arrival: Four Stages, One Advisor

Here is what each stage involves and how Study House supports you through it.

  1. 01 University Application
  2. 02 Scholarship & Funding
  3. 03 Student Visa
  4. 04 Relocation & First Weeks

01 University Application

Without Support
  • ✗ A template application copied from the internet — the committee spends ~8 minutes on it and has seen a hundred just like it.
  • ✗ A personal statement not tailored to the specific department — a formal rejection 2–3 months later.
  • ✗ Applying to just 1–2 universities at random, with no backup options.
  • ✗ A missed internal department or portal deadline.
  • ✗ No assessment of your chances before applying — you find out the result once other deadlines have already passed.
With Study House
  • ✓ A list of 5–7 universities matched to your profile and budget, with an honest assessment of your chances at each.
  • ✓ A personal narrative and essay built around the specific department's values, not a template.
  • ✓ A recommendation and portfolio strategy matched to the program's requirements.
  • ✓ A submission schedule for each university with buffer time before the deadline.
  • ✓ One consultant manages the entire package — you always know exactly what stage your application is at.

02 Scholarship & Funding

Without Support
  • ✗ Scholarship search limited to whatever shows up in a Google search — most listings are outdated or already closed.
  • ✗ A generic motivation letter reused across every scholarship application.
  • ✗ Missing merit-based or need-based funding because eligibility rules were never checked.
  • ✗ No backup financial plan if the scholarship falls through.
  • ✗ Applying after the priority deadline, when award pools are already smaller.
With Study House
  • ✓ A shortlist of scholarships and tuition waivers you actually qualify for, ranked by award size and odds.
  • ✓ A motivation letter and financial-need statement written for each specific fund.
  • ✓ A blocked-account and proof-of-funds plan set up in parallel, so your visa isn't held up by financing.
  • ✓ Priority-deadline tracking, so you apply while the award pool is largest.
  • ✓ One consultant coordinating scholarship, tuition, and visa financing as one plan, not three.

03 Student Visa

Without Support
  • ✗ Visa documents assembled without knowing which translations need notarization or apostille.
  • ✗ A blocked account opened at the wrong bank, or with the wrong minimum balance.
  • ✗ A consulate appointment booked without interview preparation.
  • ✗ A missing required document discovered only at the counter — weeks lost rebooking.
  • ✗ No visibility into processing time, so travel and housing get booked on guesswork.
With Study House
  • ✓ A complete, pre-checked document file — translated, notarized, and apostilled to the exact consulate standard.
  • ✓ Blocked account and proof-of-funds set up at an approved institution, at the correct amount.
  • ✓ A full pre-interview briefing before your consular appointment.
  • ✓ A document checklist confirmed complete before you ever leave for the embassy.
  • ✓ A realistic processing-time estimate, so your travel dates are booked with confidence.

04 Relocation & First Weeks

Without Support
  • ✗ Arriving with no housing booked, and searching in person while jet-lagged.
  • ✗ No bank account set up before the residence-permit appointment, which some offices require.
  • ✗ Missing the local registration deadline, risking fines or permit delays.
  • ✗ No orientation on the university's international-student office or local health-insurance rules.
  • ✗ Figuring out public transport, SIM cards, and groceries alone, in a new language.
With Study House
  • ✓ Housing shortlisted, and where possible confirmed, before you land.
  • ✓ A bank account and local registration appointment scheduled before or right after arrival.
  • ✓ Residence-permit registration handled within the required window.
  • ✓ A welcome briefing covering your university's international office, health insurance, and local essentials.
  • ✓ A local contact point for your first weeks, so questions get answered same-day, not next semester.
